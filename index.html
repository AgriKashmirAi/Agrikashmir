🍎 Apple Orchard Management

Important practices for productive and healthy orchards.

Orchard Establishment

  • Assess soil and drainage.
  • Choose suitable varieties and rootstocks.
  • Use healthy planting material.
  • Plan irrigation and orchard layout.

Pruning & Training

  • Maintain suitable tree structure.
  • Remove dead and diseased branches.
  • Allow adequate light penetration.
  • Follow variety-specific practices.

Fruit Quality

  • Maintain balanced nutrition.
  • Monitor crop load.
  • Manage irrigation.
  • Harvest at suitable maturity.

🔬 Apple Diseases & Pest Library

Recognising orchard problems is the first step towards managing them.

Apple Scab

Symptoms: Olive-green to dark spots on leaves and fruit. Fruit may develop blemishes.

Management: Orchard sanitation, monitoring and locally recommended disease management.

Powdery Mildew

Symptoms: White powder-like growth on leaves, shoots and flower clusters.

Management: Monitor new growth and follow locally approved recommendations.

Canker & Dieback

Symptoms: Sunken bark, dead shoots and damaged branches.

Management: Sanitation, suitable pruning and professional diagnosis.

Insect Pest Monitoring

Inspect leaves, shoots and fruit regularly. Use integrated pest management and protect beneficial organisms.

Important agricultural safety notice: These are educational descriptions, not confirmed diagnoses. Verify disease identification and treatment with SKUAST-Kashmir or a qualified horticulture expert. Always follow current pesticide labels and official advisories.

🌳 High-Density Plantation Guide

Planning modern and productive apple orchard systems.

01. Site Selection

Evaluate climate, soil, drainage, water availability and land suitability.

02. Rootstock & Variety

Choose suitable planting material based on location-specific technical advice.

03. Support System

Plan trellising, tree support, training and irrigation infrastructure.

04. Orchard Management

Maintain pruning, crop load, nutrition, irrigation and regular monitoring.

High-density orchard design and investment requirements vary by site and production system. Obtain a technical plan before establishing an orchard.

📅 Kashmir Orchard Calendar

A general guide to seasonal apple orchard activities.

SeasonGeneral Orchard Activities
WinterOrchard sanitation, dormant pruning when suitable and planning.
Early SpringBud monitoring, orchard inspection and pollination planning.
SpringFlowering observation, fruit set assessment and disease monitoring.
SummerIrrigation, crop load management and pest monitoring.
AutumnHarvesting, grading, storage preparation and orchard records.
Actual timing depends on altitude, weather, variety and local orchard conditions. This is not a spray schedule.
