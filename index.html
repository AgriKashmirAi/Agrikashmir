Seasonal awareness

Kashmir Farming Calendar

A general overview of seasonal agricultural activities.

January – March

Orchard inspection, winter protection and planning for pruning and spring operations.

April – June

Flowering, pollination, orchard monitoring and early-season crop management.

July – September

Fruit development, irrigation management, pest monitoring and harvest preparation.

October – December

Harvest completion, orchard sanitation, soil management and winter preparation.

Important: These are general seasonal guidelines. Actual activities depend on crop variety, altitude, weather and local agricultural advisories. Consult an agricultural expert before treatment or major farm operations.
